# Surface textures

All from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/textures) (CC0, public domain),
downloaded at 1K and re-encoded to WebP (`_d` colour, `_n` normal (OpenGL),
`_r` roughness at 512).

brown_mud_dry, brown_planks_05, coast_land_rocks_01, coast_sand_01,
cobblestone_floor_08, concrete_floor_02, concrete_wall_008, corrugated_iron,
dark_wooden_planks, excavated_soil_wall, lichen_rock, marble_01,
metal_grate_rusty, metal_plate_02, moon_meteor_01, muddy_tracks, rock_face,
rock_face_03, rock_wall_08, rock_wall_10, snow_02, stone_floor, wood_planks_dirt

lava_crust, lava_molten: from the lava model in the project's own assets
(assets/NEW/lava.glb), its colour, normal, roughness and glow maps, 1K WebP.
brown_mud_dry is no longer used (the Trench Front's ground is lava now).
